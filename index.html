Settings

"Catches" = share of stool samples taken in the 14 days before an infection that fall at or above the cut-off. Statistics are from the retrospective MSK allo-HCT cohort, scored by models that never saw the patient (cross-validation). Cut-offs are chosen on that same cohort.

About

Each stool sample gives two scores: the model's estimate of the risk of an E. coli and of an Enterococcus bloodstream infection (BSI) in the next 14 days. Step through a patient's stay with ‹ › (or the arrow keys); later data stay hidden.
